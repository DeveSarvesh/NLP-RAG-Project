Why do language models hallucinate and need external memory?

10:24 AM

Language models can hallucinate because they generate responses based on patterns learned from their training data, which can lead to incorrect or fabricated information when they encounter knowledge gaps. External memory helps by providing access to reliable, up-to-date information, allowing the model to ground its responses in factual sources rather than relying solely on its internal knowledge.

Key Points:

  • Hallucinations occur due to limitations in the model's training data, knowledge cutoffs, and context window. [Doc: sample_nlp_paper.pdf, Page: 1]
  • External memory (e.g., vector databases, dense retrieval systems) provides factual context to reduce hallucinations. [Doc: sample_nlp_paper.pdf, Page: 1]
  • RAG combines retrieval with generation to improve accuracy and reliability. [Doc: sample_nlp_paper.pdf, Page: 1]
